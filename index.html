Exploitation often happens in plain sight.

Human trafficking, child sexual exploitation, elder financial exploitation and predatory scams rarely look the way we expect. Many people living through them don’t recognize it at first. Kindred helps communities learn the signs.

What we focus on

Four kinds of exploitation that hide inside ordinary life. Each one has warning signs that anyone can learn.

Human trafficking

Using force, fraud or coercion to make someone work or provide commercial sex for someone else’s profit. It does not require crossing a border or being locked up, and for anyone under 18, commercial sex is trafficking even without force.

Read the warning signs

Child sexual exploitation

Any sexual abuse or exploitation of someone under 18, in person or online, including grooming and sextortion. It is most often committed by someone the child or their family knows and trusts.

Read the warning signs

Elder financial exploitation

The illegal or improper use of an older adult’s money, property or assets, most often by someone they know and trust, such as a relative, caregiver or new friend.

Read the warning signs

Predatory scams

Schemes that trick people into handing over money or personal information themselves, through fake relationships, investments, emergencies or officials. They target people of every age.

Read the warning signs

The scale of the problem

27.6 million

people were in forced labour on any given day, worldwide.

Source: ILO, Walk Free and IOM, Global Estimates of Modern Slavery (2022)

1.4 million

reports of online enticement of children in 2025, up 156% in a year.

Source: National Center for Missing & Exploited Children

$7.7 billion

reported lost to online crime by people aged 60 and over in the U.S. in 2025.

Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, via AARP

$15.9 billion

reported lost to fraud by U.S. consumers in 2025.

Source: U.S. Federal Trade Commission

Reported losses and reports are only the cases people recognized and reported. The real numbers are higher.

Could you spot it?

Our anonymous survey takes about 15 minutes. Topic by topic, you’ll read short, real-life situations, answer a few quick questions and tell us how you’d like to learn. At the end you’ll see your results and the correct answers.

Your answers help Kindred understand which myths are most common, so we can focus our education where it matters most.
