Exploitation often happens in plain sight.

Human trafficking, scams, elder financial exploitation and child sexual exploitation rarely look the way we expect. Many people living through them don’t recognize it at first. Kindred helps communities learn the signs.

What we focus on

Four kinds of exploitation that hide inside ordinary life. Each one has warning signs that anyone can learn.

Human trafficking

It often looks like a job, a relationship or a favor. Control, not chains, is the warning sign.

Read the warning signs

Scams

Romance, investment and impostor scams are built to fool careful people, using trust, fear and urgency.

Read the warning signs

Older adults and money

Most financial exploitation of older adults is committed by someone they know and trust.

Read the warning signs

Children online

Groomers often pose as a slightly older friend in the games and apps children already use.

The scale of the problem

27.6 million

people were in forced labour on any given day, worldwide.

Source: ILO, Walk Free and IOM, Global Estimates of Modern Slavery (2022)

$15.9 billion

reported lost to fraud by U.S. consumers in 2025.

Source: U.S. Federal Trade Commission

$7.7 billion

reported lost to online crime by people aged 60 and over in the U.S. in 2025.

Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, via AARP

1.4 million

reports of online enticement of children in 2025, up 156% in a year.

Source: National Center for Missing & Exploited Children

Reported losses and reports are only the cases people recognized and reported. The real numbers are higher.

Could you spot it?

Our anonymous survey takes about 15 minutes. You’ll read six short, real-life situations, answer twenty true-or-false questions and tell us how you’d like to learn. At the end you’ll see your results and the correct answers.

Your answers help Kindred understand which myths are most common, so we can focus our education where it matters most.

Kindred

Kindred is a community movement to awaken the humanity in us and help people recognize and stand against human trafficking, scams, elder financial exploitation and child sexual exploitation. It is not a crisis service, a law enforcement agency or a legal provider.
