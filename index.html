Each topic is rated on what changed during the period, based on public sources only. Sunny: no new obligation or enforcement action found in public sources. Cloudy: a new requirement was announced, or takes effect within 12 months, and providers should review their controls. Stormy: a regulator or court imposed penalties or binding obligations on a payments business, or a requirement carrying penalties takes effect within 90 days. How we rate conditions

Purpose and mission

To make the human impact of payments decisions impossible to overlook, so that awareness becomes responsibility, and responsibility becomes action.

Our mission is to help payments and compliance professionals look past labels and checklists to the real risks, through sourced analysis, practical guides and open discussion, so the people behind every transaction are better protected.

Behind every payment is a person.
