Merchant of Record or PayFac?

A free, practical guide for acquirers, PayFacs and risk teams onboarding businesses that sell on behalf of others. Informed by Visa’s merchant rules and the FTC’s action against Paddle.

Why it matters

Payments compliance can feel far removed from the people Kindred exists to protect. It isn’t. Scams that target older adults and vulnerable people still need a way to take payment. When a business that processes for many sellers is treated as a single merchant, underwriting and monitoring stop at that business and never reach the sellers behind it, or the consumers they reach.

What’s inside

  • The real risks we are mitigating, and the facts of FTC v. Paddle
  • An 11-question test: merchant, MoR or PayFac, against Visa’s merchant criteria and lessons from the case
  • Grey zones, warning signs and following the money
  • What to look for, before and after onboarding
