Get help

If you or someone you know needs help, start with the services below. Many answer around the clock and can connect you with local shelter, legal and medical support.

Outside the United States

If you are worried aboutWhere to start
Anyone in immediate dangerYour local emergency number, for example 112 across the EU and many other countries, or 999 in the UK.
Human traffickingFind a hotline in your country through the Global Modern Slavery Directory. Canada: 1-833-900-1010. UK: 08000 121 700.
A child at risk onlineReport to your national hotline through INHOPE, or find a child helpline at Child Helpline International.
Predatory scamsReport cross-border scams at econsumer.gov, and contact your bank, police or national consumer protection agency.
