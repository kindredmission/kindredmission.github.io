For survivors

Your experience matters, and you decide what to share. There are two ways to help others, both private, and you can stop at any time.

Anonymous, 10 to 15 minutesTake the survivor surveyTell us what helped, what didn’t, and what could have made a difference sooner. Share your story

How it works

  1. You fill in a short, private form. Only the site’s organizer can read it.
  2. We contact you in the way you choose, at a time that is safe for you.
  3. You decide what, if anything, is shared, and you review it first.

Before you begin

Your choices

You choose how to share (in writing, by phone, by video, in person or recorded), which topics you are comfortable discussing, and how you are named, including completely anonymously. You can withdraw at any time, including after you send the form.

Protecting your safety and privacy
  • Leave out names of people involved, exact places and dates if you are unsure.
  • If there is an open legal case, talk with your attorney or advocate before sharing details.
  • On a shared device, clear your browser history afterward. The Quick exit button at the top of every page leaves this site immediately.
If you need help now

This form is not monitored around the clock. For help now, in the U.S. call the National Human Trafficking Hotline at 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Are you 18 or older?

Thank you for reaching out. You deserve support right now.

We are not able to take stories from people under 18 through this form. Please talk to someone who can help today: call the National Human Trafficking Hotline at 1-888-373-7888, text 233733, or talk to a trusted adult. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
