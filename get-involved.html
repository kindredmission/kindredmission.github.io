Get involved

Build knowledge and join the network

Kindred is building a network of people who notice: neighbors, parents, caregivers, bank staff, nurses, teachers, drivers, hotel workers and many others who see people every day.

  • Start with the warning signs and test yourself.
  • Receive short, practical resources you can share with family and colleagues.
  • Hear first about trainings, events and ways to volunteer.

Bring training to your community

Kindred is developing practical sessions that help people recognize exploitation and respond safely. Telling us what your group needs helps us shape them.

Topics
Recognizing trafficking; scams and older adults; keeping children safe online; how to respond and where to report
For
Workplaces, schools, faith communities, community groups, banks, healthcare and hospitality teams

Kindred

Kindred is a community movement to awaken the humanity in us and help people recognize and stand against human trafficking, scams, elder financial exploitation and child sexual exploitation. It is not a crisis service, a law enforcement agency or a legal provider.
