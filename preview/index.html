Why Kindred

Behind every fraud alert and suspicious payment there is a person.

Kindred was started by a compliance professional who realized how much the people behind the numbers are missed: the worker trafficked into a scam compound, the older adult persuaded to move their savings, the teenager being extorted. Good compliance protects them.

The Compliance weather keeps professionals ahead of the rules, and every edition links to its sources so you can check our work.
