Share an idea

Prevention often starts with someone noticing a gap in their own community. We welcome ideas for awareness, prevention, survivor support, policy and research.

Questions to get you started

  • Where would a hotline number be seen at the right moment, such as rest stop restrooms, bus stations, clinics or motels?
  • Who in your community sees people every day and should know the warning signs, such as hotel staff, drivers, nurses, teachers or landlords?
  • What would have helped someone get support sooner?

Send your idea

Ideas are reviewed by the organizer before anything is shared on this site. Please don’t include personal stories or identifying details here. Survivors can use the private story form instead.

Kindred

Kindred is a community movement to awaken the humanity in us and help people recognize and stand against human trafficking, child sexual exploitation, elder financial exploitation and predatory scams. It is not a crisis service, a law enforcement agency or a legal provider.
